## SALAD

### 1. Sprouted Moong Chaat Bowl
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** No-cook | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Regular chaat swaps fried sev/puri for sprouted moong + roasted peanuts — same tang, way more protein and fiber
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 228 cal | 10g protein | 38g carbs | 5g fat | 7g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Sprouted moong — 1/2 cup (75g)
- Boiled potato — 1/2 medium, diced
- Onion — 1/4 small, finely chopped
- Tomato — 1/2 medium, deseeded and diced
- Cucumber — 1/2 small, diced
- Roasted peanuts — 1 tbsp
- Chaat masala — 1/2 tsp
- Lemon — 1.5 tsp juice
- Coriander leaves — 1 tbsp, chopped
- Salt — to taste

**Process**
1. If not pre-sprouted, soak moong overnight and let it sprout for 1-2 days (see prep-ahead).
2. In a bowl, combine sprouted moong, potato, onion, tomato, and cucumber.
3. Add roasted peanuts, chaat masala, and salt.
4. Squeeze lemon juice over the mix and toss well.
5. Garnish with coriander and serve immediately (don't dress ahead of time or it goes soggy).

- **Prep-ahead:** Sprout the moong 1-2 days ahead (soak 8 hrs, drain, leave in a covered bowl at room temp, rinsing once). Dice onion/tomato/cucumber the night before and refrigerate.

### 2. Paneer Tikka Salad
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Air-fryer | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Restaurant tikka minus the heavy oil — air-fried instead of pan-fried in ghee, served over greens instead of naan
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 357 cal | 22g protein | 12g carbs | 25g fat | 3g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Paneer — 100g, cubed
- Hung curd (yogurt) — 3 tbsp
- Tikka masala — 3/4 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste — 1/2 tsp
- Bell pepper — 1/2 cup, cubed
- Onion — 1/4 small, cubed (for skewering)
- Mixed greens/lettuce — 1 cup
- Mint chutney — 1 tbsp
- Oil — 1/2 tsp (for air-fryer basket)

**Process**
1. Mix hung curd, tikka masala, ginger-garlic paste, and a pinch of salt in a bowl.
2. Add paneer, bell pepper, and onion cubes; coat well. Marinate at least 30 min (ideally overnight).
3. Thread onto skewers, or place directly in the air-fryer basket lightly greased with the oil.
4. Air-fry at 200°C for 12-15 minutes, turning halfway, until edges char slightly.
5. Plate the greens, top with the air-fried paneer and peppers.
6. Drizzle mint chutney over the top and serve warm.

- **Prep-ahead:** Marinate paneer + veggies the night before. Wash and store greens in an airtight container. Skewer and air-fry fresh.

### 3. Chicken Tikka Protein Salad
- **Diet:** Non-veg | **Method:** Air-fryer | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Same tikka marinade logic with chicken breast — high protein, low effort after a long commute
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 236 cal | 37g protein | 8g carbs | 6g fat | 1g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Chicken breast — 150g, cubed
- Hung curd — 3 tbsp
- Tikka masala — 3/4 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste — 1/2 tsp
- Lemon juice — 1.5 tsp
- Cucumber — 1/2 small, sliced
- Onion — 1/4 small, thinly sliced
- Mixed greens — 1 cup
- Mint-yogurt dip — 1 tbsp (curd + mint + salt)

**Process**
1. Combine hung curd, tikka masala, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and salt.
2. Add chicken cubes, coat thoroughly, and marinate at least 2 hours (ideally overnight, in the fridge).
3. Air-fry at 200°C for 15-18 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until cooked through (internal temp 75°C).
4. Toss greens, cucumber, and onion in a bowl.
5. Top with the air-fried chicken tikka pieces.
6. Serve with mint-yogurt dip on the side.

- **Prep-ahead:** Marinate chicken the night before. Slice cucumber/onion the night before.

## ROLL

### 4. Beetroot Kathi Roll (Paneer Bhurji)
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Stovetop | **Serves:** 1 (2 rolls)
- **Twist:** Beetroot-kneaded roti instead of plain maida roti — natural color, extra fiber, no maida
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 690 cal | 31g protein | 79g carbs | 30g fat | 13g fiber ⚠️ heavier than expected — see note below

**Ingredients**
*For the roti:*
- Whole wheat flour — 3/4 cup (~90g)
- Beetroot — 1/2 small, boiled and pureed (~2 tbsp puree)
- Water — as needed to knead
- Salt — a pinch

*For the filling:*
- Paneer — 100g, crumbled
- Onion — 1/2 medium, chopped
- Capsicum — 1/4, chopped
- Tomato — 1/2, chopped
- Roll/pav bhaji masala — 1/2 tsp
- Oil — 1.5 tsp

**Process**
1. Knead whole wheat flour with beetroot puree, a pinch of salt, and water as needed into a soft dough. Rest 15 min.
2. Roll into 2 thin rotis and cook on a hot tawa until light brown spots appear on both sides.
3. Heat oil in a pan, sauté onions until translucent, add capsicum and tomato, cook 3-4 min.
4. Add crumbled paneer and roll masala, mix well, cook 3-4 min until combined.
5. Spoon the bhurji filling onto each roti, roll tightly, and serve warm.

- **Prep-ahead:** Knead the beetroot dough the night before and refrigerate. Chop onion/capsicum/tomato the night before.

### 5. Egg Bhurji Roll, Spinach Roti
- **Diet:** Egg | **Method:** Stovetop | **Serves:** 1 (2 rolls)
- **Twist:** Spinach-pureed roti sneaks in greens, wrapped around a light egg bhurji
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 488 cal | 25g protein | 69g carbs | 14g fat | 11g fiber

**Ingredients**
*For the roti:*
- Whole wheat flour — 3/4 cup (~90g)
- Spinach — 1/2 cup, blanched and pureed
- Water — as needed
- Salt — a pinch

*For the filling:*
- Eggs — 2, beaten
- Onion — 1/4 small, finely chopped
- Green chili — 1/2, chopped
- Turmeric — a pinch
- Oil — 1/2 tsp

**Process**
1. Knead flour with spinach puree and a pinch of salt into a soft dough. Rest 15 min, roll into 2 rotis, and cook on a tawa.
2. Heat oil in a pan, sauté onion and green chili until soft.
3. Add turmeric and beaten eggs, scramble on medium heat until just cooked.
4. Spoon the egg bhurji onto each roti, roll, and serve warm.

- **Prep-ahead:** Spinach roti dough can be made the night before and refrigerated. Chop onion the night before.

### 6. Chana Patty Roll (Chickpea Burger Filling as a Roll)
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Air-fryer | **Serves:** 1 (2 rolls, 4 patties — extra patties freeze well)
- **Twist:** The chickpea-patty-instead-of-meat trick, wrapped in a roti instead of a bun
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 618 cal | 26g protein | 118g carbs | 7g fat | 23g fiber ⚠️ heavier than expected — see note below

**Ingredients**
- Boiled chickpeas — 3/4 cup
- Rolled oats — 3 tbsp, ground coarsely
- Onion — 1/4 small, finely chopped
- Garlic — 1 clove, minced
- Cumin powder — 1/2 tsp
- Red chili powder — a pinch
- Whole wheat rotis — 2
- Mint chutney — 1 tbsp
- Onion (for topping) — 1/4 small, thinly sliced
- Oil spray — for air-fryer

**Process**
1. Mash chickpeas roughly (leave some texture), mix with ground oats, onion, garlic, cumin, chili powder, and salt.
2. Shape into 4 small patties. Refrigerate 20 min to firm up.
3. Air-fry at 190°C for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and firm.
4. Warm the rotis, spread mint chutney, place patties in each, top with sliced onion, and roll.

- **Prep-ahead:** Shape and refrigerate patties the night before, or batch-make on the weekend and freeze (thaw before air-frying).

## ONE-POT RICE

### 7. Masala Millet Khichdi
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** One-pot | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Millet instead of white rice — lower GI, more fiber, same comfort-food feel
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 600 cal | 23g protein | 102g carbs | 11g fat | 18g fiber ⚠️ heavier than expected — see note below

**Ingredients**
- Foxtail or barnyard millet — 1/2 cup
- Moong dal (split) — 1/4 cup
- Mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas) — 1/2 cup, chopped
- Cumin seeds — 1/4 tsp
- Turmeric — a pinch
- Ghee — 1.5 tsp
- Water — 1.75 cups
- Salt — to taste

**Process**
1. Wash and soak millet and moong dal together for at least 30 min (overnight is better).
2. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker or pot, add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
3. Add chopped vegetables, sauté 2 min.
4. Add drained millet-dal mix, turmeric, salt, and water.
5. Pressure cook for 3-4 whistles (or simmer covered for 20-25 min, stirring occasionally, until soft).
6. Serve hot, with a small drizzle of ghee on top if desired.

- **Prep-ahead:** Soak millet + dal overnight. Pre-chop vegetables the night before.

### 8. Egg Fried Brown Rice, Indian-Spiced
- **Diet:** Egg | **Method:** One-pot | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Brown rice instead of white, extra egg-to-rice ratio for protein, minimal oil
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 485 cal | 17g protein | 67g carbs | 16g fat | 5g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Cooked brown rice (day-old, chilled) — 1.25 cups
- Eggs — 1-2
- Spring onion — 2 tbsp, chopped (whites and greens separated)
- Carrot — 1/4 cup, finely diced
- Soy sauce — 1.5 tsp
- Garam masala — a pinch
- Oil — 1.5 tsp

**Process**
1. Heat oil in a wok or wide pan on high heat.
2. Add spring onion whites and carrot, stir-fry 2 min.
3. Push veggies to one side, pour beaten egg into the empty space, scramble until just set.
4. Add the chilled brown rice, breaking up any clumps.
5. Add soy sauce and garam masala, toss everything together on high heat for 3-4 min.
6. Garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot.

- **Prep-ahead:** Cook brown rice the night before and refrigerate. Dice carrot and spring onion the night before.

### 9. Chicken Keema Rice, One-Pot
- **Diet:** Non-veg | **Method:** One-pot | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Lean chicken keema instead of mutton, brown rice instead of white, cooked together for minimal cleanup
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 625 cal | 33g protein | 81g carbs | 18g fat | 6g fiber ⚠️ heavier than expected — see note below

**Ingredients**
- Minced chicken (keema) — 125g
- Brown rice — 1/2 cup
- Onion — 1/2 medium, chopped
- Tomato — 1/2 medium, chopped
- Green peas — 2 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste — 1/2 tsp
- Garam masala — 1/2 tsp
- Oil — 1.5 tsp
- Water — 1 cup

**Process**
1. Heat oil in a pressure cooker or pot, sauté onions until golden.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste, cook 1 min, then add tomato and cook until soft.
3. Add chicken keema, garam masala, and salt; cook until the keema changes color (5-6 min).
4. Add washed brown rice, peas, and water.
5. Pressure cook for 3-4 whistles, or simmer covered for 25-30 min until rice is tender.
6. Rest 5 min before serving.

- **Prep-ahead:** Marinate keema with ginger-garlic paste and garam masala the night before. Chop onion/tomato the night before.

## MARINADE (protein-prep focused — reusable across the week)

### 10. Tandoori Chicken Marinade
- **Diet:** Non-veg | **Method:** Air-fryer or oven | **Serves:** 1
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 228 cal | 35g protein | 2g carbs | 8g fat

**Ingredients**
- Chicken (breast or thigh) — 150g
- Thick curd/yogurt — 3 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste — 1.5 tsp
- Tandoori masala — 1 tsp
- Lemon juice — 1.5 tsp
- Mustard oil (or any oil) — 1/2 tsp
- Red chili powder — a pinch
- Salt — to taste

**Process**
1. Make small cuts in the chicken pieces to let the marinade penetrate.
2. Mix curd, ginger-garlic paste, tandoori masala, lemon juice, oil, chili powder, and salt into a smooth marinade.
3. Coat the chicken thoroughly, cover, and refrigerate at least 4 hours (overnight is best).
4. Air-fry at 200°C for 18-20 minutes, turning halfway, until charred at the edges and cooked through — OR oven-roast at 220°C for 20-25 min.
5. Rest 5 min before serving.

- **Prep-ahead:** Marinate the night before, or batch-marinate on the weekend and freeze in portions.

### 11. Soya Chaap Marinade
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Air-fryer or stovetop | **Serves:** 1
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 261 cal | 30g protein | 14g carbs | 11g fat

**Ingredients**
- Soya chaap (stick or malai) — 150g
- Thick curd — 3 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste — 1.5 tsp
- Chaap/tikka masala — 1 tsp
- Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) — 1/2 tsp, crushed
- Oil — 1/2 tsp
- Salt — to taste

**Process**
1. If using stick chaap, slice into bite-size pieces.
2. Mix curd, ginger-garlic paste, chaap masala, kasuri methi, oil, and salt into a marinade.
3. Coat chaap pieces well, refrigerate at least 2 hours (overnight for best flavor).
4. Air-fry at 190°C for 12-15 min, turning halfway — OR pan-sear on a hot tawa, 8-10 min, turning occasionally, until charred spots form.

- **Prep-ahead:** Marinate the night before. Soya chaap also freezes well pre-marinated.

## NOODLES / PASTA

### 12. Veggie-Loaded Whole Wheat Pasta
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Stovetop | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Whole wheat pasta instead of refined, sauce bulked with pureed vegetables instead of heavy cream
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 450 cal | 16g protein | 77g carbs | 10g fat | 12g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Whole wheat pasta — 100g
- Tomato — 1.5 medium, pureed
- Bell pepper — 1/4, diced
- Zucchini — 1/4, diced
- Garlic — 1.5 cloves, minced
- Dried herbs (oregano, basil) — 1/2 tsp
- Olive oil — 1.5 tsp
- Salt and pepper — to taste

**Process**
1. Boil pasta in salted water per package instructions until al dente. Reserve 2 tbsp pasta water, then drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté garlic until fragrant.
3. Add tomato puree, cook 5-6 min until it thickens slightly.
4. Add bell pepper and zucchini, cook 4-5 min until just tender.
5. Add dried herbs, salt, and pepper. Stir in the reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
6. Toss the cooked pasta into the sauce and mix well. Serve hot.

- **Prep-ahead:** Chop all vegetables the night before. The sauce base can be made ahead and refrigerated up to 2 days.

### 13. Schezwan Egg Noodles, Lighter Version
- **Diet:** Egg | **Method:** Stovetop | **Serves:** 1
- **Twist:** Whole wheat noodles, less oil than street-style, extra egg and veg for protein/fiber
- **Macros (per serving, calculated):** 517 cal | 19g protein | 79g carbs | 14g fat | 10g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Whole wheat noodles — 100g
- Egg — 1, beaten
- Cabbage — 1/2 cup, shredded
- Carrot — 1/4 cup, julienned
- Spring onion — 1 tbsp, chopped
- Schezwan sauce (light/homemade) — 1 tbsp
- Oil — 1.5 tsp

**Process**
1. Boil noodles per package instructions until just done, drain, and toss with a few drops of oil to prevent sticking.
2. Heat oil in a wok, scramble the egg lightly, remove and set aside.
3. In the same wok, stir-fry cabbage and carrot on high heat for 2-3 min (keep it crunchy).
4. Add boiled noodles, schezwan sauce, and the scrambled egg back in. Toss everything together for 2 min.
5. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.

- **Prep-ahead:** Shred cabbage and julienne carrot the night before.

## SNACK (batch-prepped — makes several portions, eaten across the week)

### 14. Sattu Protein Ladoo
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** No-cook | **Makes:** ~12 ladoos (1 ladoo = 1 portion)
- **Twist:** Roasted channa sattu + jaggery instead of sugar — batch-friendly, no refined sugar
- **Macros (per ladoo, calculated):** 134 cal | 4g protein | 17g carbs | 6g fat | 2g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Roasted channa sattu — 1.5 cups
- Jaggery — 1/2 cup, grated or powdered
- Ghee — 3-4 tbsp, melted
- Cardamom powder — 1/2 tsp

**Process**
1. Dry-roast the sattu lightly for 2-3 min if not already roasted. Let cool.
2. Mix sattu, grated jaggery, and cardamom powder in a large bowl.
3. Add melted ghee gradually, mixing with your hands until the mixture holds together when pressed.
4. Roll into 12 ladoos (about 20g each).
5. Store in an airtight container.

- **Prep-ahead:** Make a full batch on the weekend — stores at room temp ~5 days, or refrigerated ~2 weeks.

### 15. Roasted Makhana Chaat
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** Stovetop (light roast) | **Makes:** 2 portions
- **Twist:** Fox nuts roasted in minimal ghee with chaat masala instead of fried namkeen
- **Macros (per portion, calculated):** 231 cal | 6g protein | 32g carbs | 9g fat | 6g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Makhana (fox nuts) — 2 cups
- Ghee — 1 tbsp
- Chaat masala — 1 tsp
- Turmeric — 1/4 tsp
- Roasted peanuts — 2 tbsp
- Salt — to taste

**Process**
1. Heat ghee in a pan on low-medium heat.
2. Add makhana and roast, stirring continuously, for 8-10 min until crisp (should sound hollow when tapped).
3. Add turmeric, chaat masala, and salt, tossing to coat evenly.
4. Mix in roasted peanuts, let cool completely.
5. Store in an airtight container once fully cooled (keeps them crisp).

- **Prep-ahead:** Batch-roast on the weekend, stores ~1 week.

## DESSERT

### 16. Ragi Chocolate Energy Bites
- **Diet:** Veg | **Method:** No-cook | **Makes:** ~14 bites (1 bite = 1 portion)
- **Twist:** Ragi flour + dates + cocoa instead of maida + refined sugar — doubles as a pre-workout bite
- **Macros (per bite, calculated):** 60 cal | 1g protein | 12g carbs | 2g fat | 2g fiber

**Ingredients**
- Ragi (finger millet) flour, lightly roasted — 1/2 cup
- Dates — 1 cup, pitted
- Cocoa powder (unsweetened) — 2 tbsp
- Peanut butter — 2 tbsp
- Chia seeds — 1 tbsp
- Water — 1-2 tbsp, if needed

**Process**
1. Dry-roast ragi flour on low heat for 3-4 min until fragrant. Let cool.
2. Soak dates in warm water for 10 min if dry/hard, then drain.
3. Blend dates, cocoa powder, and peanut butter into a thick paste.
4. Mix in roasted ragi flour and chia seeds until well combined and moldable.
5. Roll into 14 small balls (~15g each).
6. Refrigerate at least 30 min to firm up before serving.

- **Prep-ahead:** Batch-make on the weekend, stores refrigerated ~1 week.
